DragGrab anywhere along the boundary (a 9 pt strip). Both columns follow the pointer; nothing else moves.
LimitsEach column has a min and a max, and the other column keeps its min. At a limit the handle turns peach and the cursor turns one-way.
Double-clickPuts the automatic width back (the one the screen computes today) and forgets the saved width.
RememberedPer screen, across restarts, in Distill’s own UI preferences (like Group by and Sort in To do). Not in settings.json; the core does not change.
Window resizeA saved width is squeezed to fit, never rewritten: make the window wide again and your width comes back. Only a drag saves.
Narrow windowToday’s rules stay: below 780 pt Review moves the Conversation behind a button, Activity opens the detail in place below 920 pt, Collectors’ log takes the window below 760 pt.
No collapseDragging never hides a column. Hiding stays with the narrow-window rules above.
VoiceOverThe handle is a “Resize” control: increment and decrement move it 20 pt.
