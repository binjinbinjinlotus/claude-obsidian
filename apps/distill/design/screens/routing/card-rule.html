YOURS → TO CONFIRMThe item’s owner (the person who has to do it) is one of that type’s people: it goes to that type’s To confirm. The row says “for Aditya” when the owner isn’t you.
OWED TO YOU → PENDINGSomeone else has to do something for you (send you a message, a link, a review): it goes to Pending, grouped by person, with the date if one was said.
EVERYONE ELSE’S → HIGHLIGHTSSomeone else’s job, not owed to you: it goes to that note’s Highlights, under Others’ actions. It never fills your lists. Anyone not in your People list → Highlights.
OWNER UNCLEAR → ASKWhen Distill can’t tell whose it is, it goes to To confirm with “Whose is this?” and three choices: You · Someone (pick) · Not mine.
